Everything you need to know about reserving, modifying, and boarding AeroLuxe international flights.

Upon completing your reservation, your digital boarding pass is generated immediately with a unique PNR code. You can print it as an airline-standard PDF ticket, access it at any time in your "My Bookings" portal, or present it on your mobile device at airport check-in.

All international bookings include 1 carry-on cabin bag (up to 10kg) and 1 personal item. Checked baggage depends on cabin class: Economy includes 1x 23kg bag; Business includes 2x 32kg bags; First Class includes 3x 32kg bags with priority priority carousel delivery.
